## Context

What is the problem or force that requires a decision? Keep it factual: the
constraints, the requirements from `PROJECT_SPEC.md`, and anything that makes the
choice non-obvious. If this decision deviates from the spec, say so explicitly.

## Decision

The choice made, stated plainly and in the active voice ("We will …"). Be
specific enough that a reader can act on it without guessing.

## Alternatives

The other options that were on the table, each named.

## Reason

Why the chosen option wins over those alternatives — the affirmative case.

## Trade-offs

What we give up or the risks we accept by choosing this. If this deviates from
`PROJECT_SPEC.md`, the trade-off of diverging belongs here.

## Consequences

What follows from the decision: downstream effects, operational implications, and
any follow-up work it creates.
